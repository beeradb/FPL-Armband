HOW TO READ THE LAST THREE COLUMNS.
  ANTI - RES = -cov(dX, sign dR) + mu*(1-2p), per offered package.
  The first term is what the run is for; the second is the nuisance
  the accept-all arm exists to identify. `ratio` is the first over
  the second: near zero means the contrast is almost entirely the
  offset and cannot discriminate, and large means the offset is
  negligible and the contrast may be read against ~zero.
  *** STOP AND REPORT, do not read the contrast, if p is far from
  0.5 AND mu is materially positive AND the ratio is near 1 or
  below. *** That is the configuration in which mass asymmetry
  alone manufactures the signature that is supposed to mean dR
  carries information about dX.
  These are PER PACKAGE. The sweep's means are per gameweek played,
  so do not add them together; what transports is the ratio.
  The arms do not share a stream — paths diverge from week one — so
  read the shipped row as the reference and the spread across rows
  as how much the divergence moves the quantities themselves.
  *** `p` HERE IS THE PACKAGE MASS AND IS THE ONE TO QUOTE. *** The
  mediator's moves(RESID)/moves(NOGATE) is a MOVE-weighted proxy for
  it — a funded pair is one package and two to five moves, and the
  singles loop ends a week on its first rejection, so a refusing arm
  loses the rest of the week's moves for reasons unrelated to its
  accept mass. Report both; the null is built from this one.
