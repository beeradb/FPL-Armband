Does residual noise scale as 1 / weeks played?
----------------------------------------------
Residuals are the season-by-start interaction terms, pooled over arms and
metrics. The null the design projections assume is a slope of -1 on
log(variance) against log(weeks), i.e. SE proportional to 1/sqrt(weeks).

Read that slope as an upper bound on the length effect, not a measurement of
it. Window length and information regime are the same variable in this
design — a 13-gameweek window is also a GW26 entry — and CLAUDE.md already
records the middle regime as noisier than its length explains. The rms
column is not monotone in weeks either (33 gameweeks reads above 38), so
treat p = 1 as the defensible assumption and 0.84 as the pessimistic one.

What each comparison can detect, per arm
----------------------------------------
'significant' is the effect that would land exactly at p = 0.05; 'MDE' is the effect
this design would detect 80% of the time. Points per season is x38 on the per_gw scale.

the licence to read the left-hand pair and a large one is *not* licence to
read the right-hand pair: at 6 seasons and 6 start points that test has only

for is there. Report the range and the evidence; do not pick an end.

What disjoint evaluation windows would buy
------------------------------------------
Residual variance taken as proportional to weeks^-p, calibrated at the
current design's mean window of 25.5 gameweeks. Two values
of p: the 1/weeks the length argument predicts, and the 0.84 measured above.

The key arithmetic: a disjoint design evaluates each season's football
exactly once, where the current nested design evaluates 4.0 seasons' worth
per season by overlapping. At p = 1 the number of disjoint windows drops
out entirely — averaging W windows of 38/W gameweeks is averaging the same
season either way — so W only ever changes the cluster count.

How much overlap there is, in shared gameweeks between entry points:
               1    6   11   16   21   26
  GW1         38   33   28   23   18   13
  GW6         33   33   28   23   18   13
  GW11        28   28   28   23   18   13
  GW16        23   23   23   23   18   13
  GW21        18   18   18   18   18   13
  GW26        13   13   13   13   13   13

Every window is a suffix of the season, so they are strictly nested: the
GW1 and GW6 entries share 33 of 38 gameweeks and the GW26 window sits
inside all five others. Building disjoint windows needs an `EndGW` that
`SimConfig` does not have — it carries `StartGW` only, and every path runs
to GW38.

Wild cluster bootstrap: the Rademacher floor at four clusters
-------------------------------------------------------------
With 6 clusters there are 2^6 = 64 Rademacher sign vectors, and the
statistic is symmetric under a global flip, so the bootstrap distribution
has at most 32 distinct absolute values. The original sample is one of them,
so the smallest attainable two-sided p is 2/64 = 0.031.

That is above 0.05, so a Rademacher wild cluster bootstrap at 6 clusters can
never reject at the 5% level however large the effect. It must not arbitrate anything here.
