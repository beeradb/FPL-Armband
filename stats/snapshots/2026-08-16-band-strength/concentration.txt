3 of 3 arms flagged.
CONCENTRATED  = |mean| >= 8 a season and under 35% survives dropping the three largest cells.
MOST CELLS DISAGREE = |mean| >= 8 a season and under 60% of cells share its sign.
`seas` = how many DISTINCT seasons the three carrier cells sit in.

(!) Neither flag is a verdict, and a lumpy effect is not a false one -- a
setting that only acts through a wildcard or a chip week SHOULD be lumpy,
and averaging it over n cells is the wrong thing to do to it. A flag says
the mean is a few events, so the follow-up is to find the right
denominator and name a mechanism that predicts those cells.

(!) Do NOT read seas=3 as evidence of a real mechanism. Three carriers in
three distinct seasons is the MODAL outcome of pure noise (P = 0.605 on a
6x6 grid). Only seas=1 is informative (P ~ 0.015). Carrier selection is
also length-biased toward the short cells at GW21/GW26, whose per-gameweek
differences carry ~1.7x the sd of a GW1 cell. See this file's header.

(!) Counts above are CONTRASTS, not distinct arms: the BLEND and BLENDLO
banks share byte-identical arms, so an arm can appear twice.
