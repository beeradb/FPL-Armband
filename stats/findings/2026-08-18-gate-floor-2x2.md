# The gate-floor 2×2: nothing resolves at level, the user's shape shows early, and the canary licenses the schedule

The user's "react faster early" hypothesis, measured at level: *"early season it may pay to
react faster because there's a ton of new info. By 6-10 games in things are typically more
Settled."* The transfer gate's singles floor is `max(MinGain, charge/horizon)` — 0.4 at the
shipped 0.4 / 2.0 / horizon 5, where both terms sit on the kink and **either lever lowered
alone is inert**. This 2×2 lowers both.

Pre-registered in the doc comment of `TestDiagGateFloorUnderExitLevers`
(`internal/backtest/floorx_diag_test.go`), committed at `ecb53a6` **before the first cell ran**,
after a plan review. Banked cells at `stats/snapshots/2026-08-18-gate-floor-2x2/cells/`,
provenance commit `ecb53a6`, `dirty=false`.

## What ran

Factor A charge: `FreeCost` 2.0 (shipped) vs 1.0. Factor B gain bar: `MinGain` 0.4 (shipped)
vs 0.2. All four corners under the ON corner (the override mode the prior 2×2 vetted at +73 and
the option-decay run corroborated at +97.4 on 36 cells), plus the OFF floor-drop simple at
shipped config. Grid: extended six seasons × six entry points, **36 cells per arm, 216 cells**,
`POLICY`. 22m53s, exit 0, 216 of 216 cells feasible. Contrasts by
`stats/floorx_contrasts.R`; the identity `S = A + B` is checked in the script, not assumed.

**The early-exposure columns are positive (5 of 6 cells in each), the late columns negative**
— the exact direction the user predicted: a low floor pays where the information is dense and
costs where the world has settled. Six cells per column, so no column resolves and the pooled
null is what the arithmetic says it is. The shape is a point-estimate observation, not a
verdict — and it is the shape the schedule arm exists to test.

## The canary: the late half is LIVE, so a level null does not bind the schedule

The pre-registered conditional, verbatim from the design: *"If the late-half drop is INERT …
level ≈ schedule and a level null binds the schedule. If the late-half drop is LIVE AND
NEGATIVE, the level can be null while the schedule helps."* The discriminator was the flip
split at GW28: **`floor_flips_gt28` is live in 6 of 6 seasons in the floor-drop arms** (and in
the {1.0,0.4} arm; the baseline and the {2.0,0.2} arm read 0 of 6, as the kink arithmetic
requires). The late half is live and its point estimates are negative — the exact case where
the level null does not bind. **The scheduled-floor arm is therefore licensed by the
pre-registered canary reading**, even though the pre-registered license S resolving did not
fire — both readings are stated rather than conflated.

## Liveness and mediators

- **flips: 6 of 6 seasons in both halves** in the floor-drop arms; the {1.0,0.4} arm also
  flips 6/6 both halves (its GW35+ horizon regime). The pre-registered floor (4 of 6) passes.
- **{2.0,0.2} is byte-identical**: 0 flips, 0 of 36 moves differ, `free_at_decision`
  unchanged — the kink identity extended to 36 cells under the ON corner: at the shipped
  charge, MinGain below 0.4 never binds anything, on this grid. The min_gain invariance,
  measured anew.
- **moves differ**: {1.0,0.4} 24/36, {1.0,0.2} ON 30/36, OFF 29/36 — the pre-registered
  floor (24) passes.
- **free_at_decision** (the spending dose): 1.77 baseline ON → 1.54 → 1.39 in the
  floor-drop arm; 1.60 → 1.26 OFF. More spending shows there.
- **banked_weeks**: max 2 in the {1.0,0.4} arm — banking, inert everywhere else (0 in all
  other 215 cells), woke up once the charge dropped. Reported per the pre-registration: a
  live third channel in that arm, of measured size ≈ nothing.
- **HOLD byte-identical** across the floor arms (SE 0) — the code fact.

## What this settles, and what it does not

**Settles**: the floor, dropped all season, is consistent with zero on this grid (−2.5
against 32.3, t −0.20; OFF −3.1 against 28.1) — a tie, not a measurement of zero. The user's
hypothesis is not supported at level. The {2.0,0.2} corner is byte-identical — MinGain below
the kink is inert at the shipped charge on the full grid (the kink arithmetic covers the
singles gate; the pair channel's zero is measured, not required).

**Does not settle**: the shape — the entry-point columns point exactly the predicted way, at
6 cells each, and the canary says the late half is live, so the scheduled floor is the
unmeasured quantity and the level null does not dispose of it. The schedule arm —
{1.0, 0.2} through GW8, shipped after, under the ON corner, pre-registered in the same doc
comment — is the follow-up.
