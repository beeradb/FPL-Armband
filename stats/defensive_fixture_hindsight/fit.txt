CORRECTIONS TO THE PRE-REGISTRATION (2026-08-16, POST-FIT)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A pre-registration edited after its fit is no longer one, so nothing below was
applied in place. These are the review findings this run verified independently.

1. SECTION 7's SIZING WAS AN APPROXIMATION AND IT DECIDED THE VERDICT.
   `(b2-1)/q_w` is an OLS omitted-variable formula, applied to a log-link GLM
   without partialling w1 or using IWLS weights. It reads 1.977; the quantity
   that sentence names is 1.685. The threshold is 1.702. So the canary FIRES
   and the verdict is C, where the approximation gave D. The error ran in the
   direction that flatters the instrument.

2. THE MECHANISM ARGUMENT IS NARROWER THAN 'UNVERIFIABLE'. The archive's own
   difficulty column is END-STAMPED, and that is measurable from the committed
   join -- see the join's liveness output. What stays unverifiable is whether
   FPL's LIVE difficulty moved in-season, which needs a fixtures payload.

3. SECTION 9's 'nothing here reaches the scored path' IS WRONG AS MECHANISM.
   `FPL_DEF_FIXTURE_SCALE` runs through defenceMultiplier -> fixtureMultipliersFor
   -> fixtureSensitiveAt and is live on the scored path. What the record holds
   is that MOVING it does not resolve on points, which is a tie and not a
   refutation. Read section 9 as 'nothing here licenses moving a scored constant'.

4. TWO ALTERNATIVES SECTION 2 DOES NOT NAME.
   (a) TIMING. Revisions land in 2-4 discrete gameweeks a season, so revised_opp
       is partly 'early in the season': mean gw 18.08 revised against 22.76
       unrevised on the native stratum. XGC90's reliability rises through the
       season, so the w2 slope can differ by calendar position with no hindsight
       involved. Unsigned: it can mask a leak as easily as manufacture one.
   (b) OUTCOME CONDITIONING. revised_opp is a function of END-of-season strength,
       which FPL sets from results including this row's own match, so b3 = 0 is
       not a clean null. Bounded at ~1 match in 38 and blunted by a 5-level scale.

5. THE ONLY DIRECT OBSERVATION OF DIFFICULTY MOVEMENT IN THIS PROJECT RUNS
   AGAINST THE HYPOTHESIS, and the pre-registration quoted the other half of
   its paragraph without it: the banked cs_calibration output records 'two live
   captures three days apart show 0 of 380 difficulties changing'. That window
   is pre-season and reaches no archived season, so it is weak -- but it was
   known beforehand and omitting it was selective.

6. SECTION 6's STATED LIVENESS BAR CANNOT FIRE. The join copies `def` unchanged
   and fails on any unmatched row, so conditional on the row count the `def`
   distribution is bit-identical BY CONSTRUCTION. The check that can fire, and
   does pass, is the independent re-derivation in the join's liveness output.
