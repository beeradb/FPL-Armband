What you get

A number on its own is not an answer. You get the number and the working.

Most tools give you a ranking. This one gives you the ranking, what’s underneath it, and a switch to overrule it when you know something the data doesn’t.

Drag the pitch, keep it legal

You can’t build an illegal eleven by accident. Try a fourth forward and it won’t take — and it’ll tell you why. Bench order is drag-to-rank, not a dropdown.
